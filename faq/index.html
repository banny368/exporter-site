Buyer FAQs

The questions a first container raises

If your question is not here, ask it directly — you will get a straight answer rather than a brochure.

Common questions

  • What is your minimum order quantity?

    It varies by product. Dehydrated products and spices start at 5 MT, moringa powder at 1 MT, furniture at one 20ft container or 50 pieces depending on the item, and fresh produce at one reefer container. Each product page states its own MOQ. Mixed-model containers are accepted in the furniture range.

  • Which Incoterms do you work on?

    EXW, FOB, CFR and CIF as standard, and CIP on air freight consignments. FOB is the most common for first orders because it gives the buyer control of the freight. We will explain the cost split at quotation if you are unsure which suits your route.

  • What are your payment terms?

    Letter of credit at sight, or 30% TT advance with the balance against a copy of the bill of lading. Terms for repeat buyers are reviewed after the first two shipments. We do not ship on open account for a first order.

  • Can I get samples before placing an order?

    Yes. Samples are couriered for dehydrated products, spices and furniture finishes, and the sample plus courier cost is credited against your first container. Fresh produce samples are only meaningful in season and are usually handled as a trial consignment instead.

  • How long does a shipment take?

    Production or procurement runs from 7 days for fresh produce in season to 75 days for hand-carved furniture. Sea transit is separate: 4–7 days to the Gulf, 18–24 days to North West Europe, 28–32 days to the US East Coast. Both figures are stated in the quotation.

  • Do you accept third-party inspection?

    Yes, and we hold the container for it. SGS, Bureau Veritas and Intertek all inspect at our facility regularly. Inspection is at the buyer's cost and is worth it on a first order.

  • What documents come with the shipment?

    Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and then whatever the product and destination require — phytosanitary certificate, fumigation certificate, certificate of analysis, health certificate, insurance certificate, non-GMO declaration. Draft documents are sent for your approval before they are finalised.

  • Can you supply private label or custom packaging?

    Yes. Buyer-branded cartons and bags are available from 10 MT on dehydrated products, from 1,000–3,000 cartons on fresh produce depending on the format, and on any volume for furniture. Send your artwork and we will confirm the format and any tooling cost.

  • Do you ship by air?

    On fresh produce, yes — mango and lime move by air from 500 kg where a buyer needs arrival inside 48 hours. Dehydrated products and furniture ship by sea; air freight on those rarely makes commercial sense and we will say so.

  • How do I start?

    Send the product, quantity, destination port and Incoterm through the contact form or on WhatsApp. A complete inquiry is quoted within 48 hours. If we cannot supply what you need, you will hear that in the same window rather than a week later.

Still deciding whether to send an inquiry?

Send the product and quantity even if the rest is undecided. We will tell you which Incoterm suits your route and what the realistic lead time is, and you can take that to whichever supplier you choose.
