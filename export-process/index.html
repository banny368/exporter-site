How we work

Inquiry to shipment, with a timeline on every step

Nothing here is unusual — it is how a competent exporter works. It is written down so you can plan against it, and so you know at which step to chase us.

  1. 01

    Inquiry received

    Send the product, quantity, destination port and preferred Incoterm. If any of those are open, we will suggest what suits the route rather than quoting the cheapest option and letting you discover the difference on arrival.

    Expected

    Same working day

  2. 02

    Quotation and samples

    A written quotation against a named specification, with packing, loadability and lead time stated. Samples are couriered on request; sample and courier cost is credited against the first container.

    Expected

    Within 48 hours

  3. 03

    Order confirmation and PI

    A proforma invoice covering specification, quantity, price basis, payment terms, port of loading and discharge, and expected shipment window. Production starts on LC receipt or on the agreed TT advance.

    Expected

    1–2 days

  4. 04

    Procurement, production and QC

    Sourcing or manufacture against the confirmed specification, with in-process checks and third-party lab testing where the product calls for it. Buyer or third-party inspection is welcome at this stage.

    Expected

    7–60 days by product

  5. 05

    Documentation and customs clearance

    Invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary or fumigation certificate, certificate of analysis and insurance prepared as one set. Draft documents go to the buyer for approval before they are finalised.

    Expected

    3–5 days

  6. 06

    Shipment and tracking

    Container stuffed under supervision, reefer set point logged where applicable, and the B/L plus complete document set couriered or released against the agreed terms. Container number and vessel details are shared at sailing.

    Expected

    Transit by route

Document flow

Which paper moves, and when

Drafts are sent for your approval before anything is finalised, so an error is corrected in India rather than at your port.

  1. 01

    At order

    • Proforma Invoice
    • Sales Contract
  2. 02

    Before production

    • LC copy or TT advice
    • Approved specification sheet
  3. 03

    Before dispatch

    • Certificate of Analysis
    • Inspection report
  4. 04

    At shipment

    • Commercial Invoice
    • Packing List
    • Certificate of Origin
    • Phytosanitary or Fumigation Certificate
  5. 05

    After sailing

    • Bill of Lading
    • Insurance Certificate
    • Container and vessel details

Who pays for what, in plain language

If you are not sure which term suits your route, tell us the destination port and we will recommend one rather than quoting the term that flatters our price.

TermWhat it meansWe payYou pay
EXWEx WorksYou collect from our facility. We load nothing and clear nothing.Goods, packing, documentsLoading, inland transport, export clearance, freight, insurance, import duty
