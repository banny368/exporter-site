An export house built around what arrives, not what is promised

An Indian export house shipping fresh produce, dehydrated products and spices, and solid wood furniture to buyers across the Middle East, Europe, North America and Southeast Asia. Direct sourcing, in-house quality control, and a full documentation set on every shipment.

Our story

The company started as a single-product trading operation, shipping dried turmeric to one buyer in Dubai against an LC at sight. There was no pack house and no cold store — material was bought, cleaned by a third party, and stuffed at a CFS. It worked because the specification was simple and the buyer checked everything.

The limits showed up the first time a buyer asked for something specific: a curcumin percentage, then a residue report, then a packing format nobody at the cleaning yard wanted to change for one container. Bringing grading and packing in-house was less a growth decision than the only way to keep saying yes.

Cold storage came next, and with it fresh produce — mango and pomegranate first, then grapes and banana. Fresh is unforgiving: a broken cold chain is not a quality dispute, it is a total loss, and it teaches process discipline faster than any certification audit. The systems built for fresh produce are the ones now applied to every vertical.

The furniture vertical was added because buyers asked. Jodhpur and Saharanpur workshops were already exporting, but rarely to a single moisture and packing standard. Bringing them under one specification, with kiln drying and ISPM-15 handling controlled centrally, made the difference between a workshop that ships and a supplier a retailer can programme.

Infrastructure

What we own rather than rent

Controlling the grading line and the cold chain is what makes a specification change possible without a third party agreeing to it first.

Pack House

12 MT per day

Washing, grading, treatment and packing lines with weight-based sizing and a dedicated area for buyer-specific packing formats.

Cold Storage

600 MT, 4 chambers

Independently controlled chambers running from 0 °C to 14 °C so mango, pomegranate, grapes and banana can be held simultaneously at their own set points.

Dry Warehouse

1,500 MT

Humidity-controlled storage for spices, dehydrated vegetables and powders, with segregated bays to prevent aroma transfer between lots.

Fumigation & Loading Bay

4 containers per day

On-site fumigation chamber and ISPM-15 pallet handling, with a dock-level loading bay so reefers are stuffed without breaking the cold chain.

  • FSSAI
