Quality assurance

Checked at six stages, not once at the end

A single pre-shipment inspection catches problems too late to fix them. Each stage below has its own reject criteria, so material that will not meet the specification is stopped at the gate rather than reworked into a container.

Process

From grower block to sealed container

The sequence is numbered because it is a sequence: each stage depends on the one before it.

  1. 01

    Sourcing

    Grower blocks and workshops are selected on track record and audited before the first purchase. Material is bought against a named specification, not on availability.

  2. 02

    Incoming inspection

    Every lot is checked at receipt for moisture, foreign matter, size and visible defects. Lots outside specification are rejected at the gate rather than reworked into a shipment.

  3. 03

    Grading and processing

    Weight or size grading, cleaning, Sortex where applicable, and treatment appropriate to the destination. Timber is kiln dried and held at 8–12% before it reaches the packing line.

  4. 04

    Laboratory testing

    Residue, aflatoxin, moisture, microbiological and heavy metal testing at NABL-accredited laboratories, against the destination market's limits rather than a domestic minimum.

  5. 05

    Pre-shipment inspection

    A sample drawn from the packed consignment is checked against the approved specification and the buyer's sample. Third-party inspection by SGS, Bureau Veritas or Intertek is welcome and the container is held for it.

  6. 06

    Loading supervision

    Container inspected for cleanliness and odour, reefer pre-cooled and set point verified, stuffing supervised and photographed, seal number recorded on the packing list.

Certifications

What each certificate actually covers

Logos on a website prove nothing on their own. Here is what each registration means for your consignment.

  • APEDA

    APEDA

    Registration with the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority, required to export scheduled agricultural products from India.

  • FSSAI

    FSSAI Licence

    Food Safety and Standards Authority of India licence covering handling, storage and export of food products.

  • SPICES BOARD

    Spices Board

    Certificate of registration as a spice exporter, with quality certification available per consignment.

  • ISO 22000

    ISO 22000

    Food safety management system covering hazard analysis, traceability and process control across the facility.

  • HACCP

    HACCP

    Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points plan applied at every processing and packing stage.

  • GLOBALG.A.P.

    GLOBALG.A.P.

    Farm-level good agricultural practice certification, with the GRASP social module for retail programmes.

  • HALAL

    Halal

    Halal certification for consignments to the Gulf, Malaysia, Indonesia and other markets that require it.

  • PHYTO

    Phytosanitary

    Phytosanitary certificate issued per consignment for plant products, confirming freedom from quarantine pests.

Laboratory

What gets tested, and where

Testing is done at NABL-accredited laboratories against the destination market's limits, not a domestic minimum. Reports are issued before the container is sealed.

Pesticide residue

Full multi-residue panel by LC-MS/MS and GC-MS/MS, reported against EU MRL or the destination's own limits, whichever is stricter.

Aflatoxin

HPLC-FLD on spices and dried botanicals, to the EU limits of 5 ppb B1 and 10 ppb total unless your market specifies otherwise.

Microbiological

Total plate count, Salmonella, E. coli, yeast and mould on dehydrated products and powders, sampled per production lot.

Heavy metals

ICP-MS for lead, cadmium, arsenic and mercury on botanicals and powders, against EU contaminant limits.

Physical parameters

Moisture, ash, acid insoluble ash, foreign matter, size grading and defect tolerance, checked in-house and confirmed externally.

Product-specific actives

Curcumin by HPLC, capsaicin for Scoville, ASTA colour by spectrophotometer, volatile oil by Clevenger distillation, Brix by refractometer.
