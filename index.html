Capability

What actually differs between one Indian supplier and another

Not adjectives. These are the six things that decide whether a container arrives matching the specification you signed.

Direct sourcing from 300+ growers

We buy at the farm gate in the clusters each product comes from, not through a mandi agent. That is what makes traceability to a grower block possible when a buyer asks for it.

In-house pack house, 12 MT per day

Grading, washing, treatment and packing run under our own supervision rather than a rented line, so a specification change does not need a third party to agree to it.

Cold chain from field to port

Pre-cooling within hours of harvest, reefer transport to the port, and a set point logged at stuffing. The temperature record travels with the documents.

Full documentation set on every shipment

Invoice, packing list, B/L, certificate of origin, phytosanitary or fumigation certificate, certificate of analysis and insurance — prepared as one set, not chased after the vessel sails.

Third-party lab reports before dispatch

Residue, moisture, aflatoxin and microbiological testing at NABL-accredited labs, with the report issued before the container is sealed rather than after arrival.

48-hour quote turnaround

A complete inquiry — product, quantity, destination port, Incoterm — is quoted within two working days. If we cannot supply it, we say so in the same window.

Process

Inquiry to shipment, in six steps

Each step has an expected timeline, so you can plan against it rather than chase it.

Read the full process
  1. 01Same working day

    Inquiry received

    Send the product, quantity, destination port and preferred Incoterm. If any of those are open, we will suggest what suits the route rather than quoting the cheapest option and letting you discover the difference on arrival.

  2. 02Within 48 hours

    Quotation and samples

    A written quotation against a named specification, with packing, loadability and lead time stated. Samples are couriered on request; sample and courier cost is credited against the first container.

  3. 031–2 days

    Order confirmation and PI

    A proforma invoice covering specification, quantity, price basis, payment terms, port of loading and discharge, and expected shipment window. Production starts on LC receipt or on the agreed TT advance.

  4. 047–60 days by product

    Procurement, production and QC

    Sourcing or manufacture against the confirmed specification, with in-process checks and third-party lab testing where the product calls for it. Buyer or third-party inspection is welcome at this stage.

  5. 053–5 days

    Documentation and customs clearance

    Invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary or fumigation certificate, certificate of analysis and insurance prepared as one set. Draft documents go to the buyer for approval before they are finalised.

  6. 06Transit by route

    Shipment and tracking

    Container stuffed under supervision, reefer set point logged where applicable, and the B/L plus complete document set couriered or released against the agreed terms. Container number and vessel details are shared at sailing.
