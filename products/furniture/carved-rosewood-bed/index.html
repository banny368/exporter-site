About this product

The headboard is carved by hand, which is why the lead time on this piece is 60 to 75 days and why no two are line-for-line identical. Jodhpur and Saharanpur both hold carving workshops where the motifs and the tooling have not changed much in a century, and the depth of relief is what separates this from a machine-routed imitation.

Construction is traditional mortise and tenon at the corners rather than screw-and-dowel. It is slower to build and it is the reason a bed of this weight stays square after years of use. Side rails lock into the posts with steel bed bolts so the frame can be knocked down and reassembled without loosening the joinery.

King is standard at 193 × 203 cm. UK king, US queen and custom sizes are made to order at the same price band, and the carving pattern can be changed to a buyer's drawing on volumes from 20 units.
