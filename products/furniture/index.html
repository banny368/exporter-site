Wooden Furniture & Handicrafts

Solid wood furniture from Jodhpur, Saharanpur and Moradabad — Sheesham dining and bedroom, reclaimed hardwood storage, mango wood and iron, hand-woven cane. Every piece is kiln dried to 8–12% moisture before it is packed, because furniture built at Indian humidity splits in a heated European or North American home. Lead times run 35 to 75 days depending on how much of the piece is made by hand.

Shipping this range

What to know before you order

Fumigation certificate and ISPM-15 heat-treated, stamped pallets on every wooden shipment, without exception. Moisture content is controlled at 8–12% and the reading for each unit is recorded on the packing list, so a buyer can check it on arrival rather than argue about it later. Private label, custom finish and buyer-specified packaging are available. Sample orders are accepted before bulk confirmation, and the sample cost is credited against the first container.

Typical packing

Knock-down flat pack where the piece allows it, assembled where it does not. EPE foam, 5-ply corrugated, corner protectors, ISPM-15 heat-treated pallets throughout. Mixed-model containers accepted from one 20ft.
